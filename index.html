Business automation

One customer action, one connected chain of events

This is what automation looks like in practice — a single form submission triggers every downstream step, with no one at a keyboard moving it along.

Step 01

Customer submits a form

An enquiry, booking, or lead comes in from the website or WhatsApp.

Step 02

Saved to the database

No spreadsheet, no re-typing — the record exists the moment it's submitted.

Step 03

Lead scored automatically

The system flags how likely and how urgent the lead is.

Step 04

WhatsApp message sent

The customer gets an instant acknowledgment on the channel they used.

Step 05

Email confirmation sent

A written record lands in their inbox for reference.

Step 06

Staff member notified

The right person is alerted — nobody has to go looking.

Step 07

Dashboard updated

Leadership sees it reflected in real time, no reporting required.

Step 08

Follow-up task created

The next human step is queued up automatically, so nothing slips.
